Coldle

Guess the mystery peak among the most prominent summits. Each guess shows the linking col, which is the lowest point on the high route between the guessed peak and the mystery peak. The higher the linking col, the warmer you are.

Hints

  1. The mystery peak is 0,000 m high.
  2. The mystery peak has 0,000 m of prominence.
  3. The mountain range will be revealed after your third guess.
  4. The country will be revealed after your fifth guess.
